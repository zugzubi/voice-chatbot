VOICE-ENABLED CHATBOT — POTENTIAL TEACHER QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
================================================================

Q: Is this ChatGPT?
A: No — this is a custom BiLSTM trained on my own dataset in intents.json.
   It has no LLM API dependency.

Q: Why LSTM instead of a Transformer?
A: For 19 intents and ~180 patterns, a BiLSTM is the right size — a
   Transformer would overfit and wouldn't fit in the 512 MB deployment
   memory. Transformers would be Future Scope.

Q: Why 50% confidence threshold?
A: Empirical — high enough to reject noise, low enough that valid
   rephrasings still pass. Tunable in models/config.pkl.

Q: What is the dataset size?
A: 19 intents, ~180 hand-written patterns, split 70/15/15 stratified.

Q: How do you handle out-of-vocab words?
A: The Keras tokenizer uses an <OOV> token that maps unknown words to a
   single embedding index.

Q: Can it learn new intents?
A: Yes — add a new tag to intents.json, rerun train.py, redeploy.
   The retraining takes ~1 minute.
